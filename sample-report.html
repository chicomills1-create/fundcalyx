O-OJJDP-2026-172743 · Office of Juvenile Justice Delinquency Prevention · Posted Oct 9, 2026 · Closes Nov 16, 2026 · Up to $2,000,000 · ~15 awards · No cost sharing

Every published criterion satisfied. The funder's determination governs at application; the full notice controls. Official record ↗
