HOW SCREENING WORKS

Three answers. All of them honest.

Every screened opportunity gets exactly one of three outcomes — each tied to the published criteria in the funder’s official notice. FundCalyx never decides or certifies your eligibility; the funder does.

✓

Matches published criteria

Your profile satisfies every eligibility criterion the notice states. You’ll see each criterion and the passage it came from.

✕

Does not match

A stated criterion rules your profile out — and we show you which one, so no time is spent on a dead end.

?

Cannot be determined

The notice doesn’t settle it, or a question about your profile is still open. We say so plainly instead of guessing — “cannot be determined” is a real answer here.

THE DETAILS

A few things
worth knowing.

Is funding guaranteed?

No. A match is a starting point, not an eligibility determination or an award guarantee. Always verify requirements and deadlines with the original funder.

Does FundCalyx write or submit applications?

Not in the proposed initial product. It focuses on opportunity discovery, eligibility prompts, links and deadline organization.

Are the sample opportunities real?

No. The interactive preview uses illustrative records. Live data, source links and update schedules must be connected before launch.

Can I cancel a subscription?

Yes — self-service, anytime. Ordinary cancellation runs to the end of the period you paid for. If FundCalyx ever ends your service itself, the unused part of the period is refunded pro-rata. The two are different, and the refund policy says so in plain language.

Where do the grants come from?

U.S. federal opportunities, via Grants.gov. Foundation, corporate and most state grants are not in this data — FundCalyx is a federal-grants tool and says so up front. This product uses the Grants.gov API but is not endorsed or certified by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.

How does screening actually work?

We read the funder’s full official notice and check your profile against each published eligibility criterion, keeping the source passage behind every outcome. If the notice can’t settle a question, the answer is “cannot be determined” — never a guess dressed up as a verdict.

How fast are change alerts?

Monitoring runs daily, not in real time. When a sweep detects a change and verifies it, the alert is queued and sent — expect same-day on a normal cycle, and we’ll show a visible stale-data notice rather than send alerts built on a feed we couldn’t verify.

What if FundCalyx gets a deadline wrong?

We notify you promptly, publish the correction, and assess the impact. Where a correction can’t restore what was lost — a passed deadline can’t be un-passed — the affected period is credited or refunded under the refund policy. Corrections never erase the original error from our accuracy record.

Do you hold my Grants.gov or SAM.gov login?

Never. FundCalyx works from public funder data and the profile you give us. Your portal credentials are yours alone, and we will never ask for them.
